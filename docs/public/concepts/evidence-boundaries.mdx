A successful transport only proves that one request crossed the local wire.
The semantic result has its own closed outcome and evidence coordinates.

## Three closed outcomes

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Complete">
    The operation met its admitted scope, bounds, and completeness policy. The
    payload can support the full claim described by that operation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Qualified">
    The payload remains usable within a named limitation. The response keeps
    the missing coverage or reached bound attached to the evidence.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rejected">
    Kast established no successful payload. The response returns one typed
    reason from the operation's finite failure set.
  </Card>
</Columns>

The twelve hosted operations cover workspace inspection, topology publication,
three symbol operations, one-hop relation reads, bounded traversal, scoped
diagnostics, and four AddDeclaration lifecycle phases. Exact symbol resolution,
symbol description, topology publication, and each change phase require a
complete result. Workspace inspection, discovery, relation, traversal, and
diagnostic reads may return qualified evidence when the limitation is part of
their public contract.

## Every answer has coordinates

A semantic claim is valid only where all of its coordinates still hold:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Exact root">
    The canonical repository checkout that owns the admitted IDE endpoint.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Operation">
    The typed question asked, such as `workspace.inspect` or `symbol.describe`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generation">
    The semantic workspace state against which selectors and evidence were
    issued.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scope and bounds">
    The discovery mode, search scope, result limit, and completeness policy
    admitted for the request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Qualification">
    Any explicit limitation that prevents the result from supporting a
    stronger claim.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Unknown, stale, unsupported, incomplete, or unproven coordinates fail closed.
  Kast does not guess a current symbol or turn partial coverage into complete
  evidence.
</Info>

## Judge a negative answer

An empty result is strong evidence only when the result is complete for the
scope that matters. Before treating "no matches" as a repository fact, check:

- the exact root is the intended checkout
- the generation is still current
- the selected operation answers the intended question
- scope and result limits cover the decision
- the outcome is complete, not qualified or rejected

The [CLI reference](/reference/cli) lists the current operations.
[How Kast works](/explanation/how-kast-works) shows where their evidence is
admitted and projected.
