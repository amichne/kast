Live read evidence identifies the workspace and source state that support a semantic result. Its lifetime and identity apply to that read.

Retained results and continuations need their original read state. An exact symbol can undergo fresh compiler revalidation.

[Results](/reference/responses#saved-state-and-identity) defines reuse rules. [Query execution and evidence delivery](/explanation/query-lifecycle) explains their different purposes.
