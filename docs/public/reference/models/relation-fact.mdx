A relation fact records a compiler-established relationship in the admitted source domain. The producing query separately reports coverage and occurrence evidence.

Lexical containment does not alone establish a named caller. A static callback route does not prove runtime execution.

[Callback evidence](/reference/callbacks) describes ownership and deferred initializer supply. [Find and trace code](/reference/search) gives the investigation procedure.
