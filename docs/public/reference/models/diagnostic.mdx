`check_diagnostics` returns a diagnostic with its severity, message, and source location. The response records the requested scope and its coverage.

An empty list with incomplete coverage does not establish a problem-free scope. IDEA diagnostics do not establish a successful project build.

[Inspect diagnostics](/how-to/inspect-diagnostics) gives the procedure. [Change receipts](/reference/change#read-the-receipt) describe the separate source-effect evidence.
