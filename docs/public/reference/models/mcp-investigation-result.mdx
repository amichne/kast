`health_check` has its own generated readiness result, shown below. Its `complete` variant carries observation `data`; rejection carries the reported error. Do not apply that support-tool shape to `query_symbols` or `check_diagnostics`, whose structured results are their canonical operation documents.

The check observes workspace binding and readiness at the time of the call. It does not establish future query success or complete semantic coverage. See the [MCP contract](/reference/mcp-catalog) for invocation and [Results](/reference/responses) for semantic reads.
