## Schema structure and admission

Tagged variants select action-specific fields. Required defaults and discriminators remain explicit in encoded output.

Schema admission establishes shape. Workspace readiness, current identity, semantic completion, and source-change authority need their own admission.

[Query forms](/reference/api) defines supported combinations. [Lifecycles](/reference/lifecycles) identifies each subsequent owner.

## Samples and observed responses

Generated samples include valid inputs and deliberate rejection cases. Their references are placeholders rather than live workspace authority.

The examples establish neither native execution nor current compiler results. Recorded responses keep their original source revision and environment.

| `12`–`13`: bounded execution and resume | One row with a continuation, then the remaining row with complete coverage. |
| `14`–`15`: find `exactStage`, walk callers | One function, then one caller record at depth 1 within the two-hop bound. |

The capture replaces the absolute checkout prefix with `/workspace/kast`. It preserves references, offsets, identities, and qualifications.

The capture predates compact output and current complete-only execution. Its semantic continuation behavior is historical rather than a public `RESUME` contract today.

Current `RESUME` only presents produced output. Current `RUN` owns internal semantic continuation and rejects incomplete proof.

Generated shape guards examine the recorded payloads. They do not replay the original IDEA interaction.
