A diagnostic is an IDEA finding. It gives a severity, message, and place in source. The response states which scope the scan covers.

A complete scan with no findings can prove that IDEA reported none within that admitted scope. A scan that did not finish cannot prove this.

Clean IDEA diagnostics do not prove that the project builds. A change receipt proves a distinct source effect. It also records which checks took place.

[Check diagnostics](/how-to/inspect-diagnostics) gives the steps. [Evidence limits](/reference/evidence-boundaries) defines what the result can prove.
