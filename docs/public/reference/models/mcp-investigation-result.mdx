The direct `health_check` result reports workspace binding and readiness. A complete variant carries observations in `data`, while rejection carries its finite error.

Readiness belongs to the call's workspace state. It does not establish a later declaration identity, relationship, or completed query.

[MCP](/reference/mcp-catalog) defines its session and wrapper. [Lifecycles](/reference/lifecycles#workspace-preparation-lifecycle) describes preparation before semantic admission.
